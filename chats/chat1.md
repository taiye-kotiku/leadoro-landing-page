# 📄 DOCUMENT 4 — CALL SCRIPT / PITCH FRAMEWORK

---

### OPENING (First 2 minutes)

> "Thanks for jumping on, [Name]. Before I show you anything, I just want to understand your situation a bit better. Is that okay?"

*(Always ask permission — it sets a consultative tone)*

---

### DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (5–7 minutes)

Ask these in a natural conversation, not like a checklist:

1. *"Where are most of your leads coming from right now — ads, referrals, Instagram?"*
2. *"When someone sends you a WhatsApp message or fills a form, how quickly do you typically get back to them?"*
3. *"What happens to leads that come in after hours or on weekends?"*
4. *"Do you have any follow-up system in place, or is it mostly manual?"*
5. *"On average, how many inquiries would you say you get per week?"*
6. *"And roughly, what percentage of those actually convert to bookings or sales?"*

*(Listen carefully. Their answers become your pitch. Use their words back to them.)*

---

### THE PRESENTATION (5–8 minutes)

> "Okay, based on what you've told me — you're getting around [X] leads per week, response time is [Y hours], and about [Z%] are converting. That's actually really common, and the good news is it's fixable."

Then show the demo video or walk through the flow live.

> "What I build is a system that sits on top of your WhatsApp and handles everything from the first message to the booked appointment. Your team only gets involved when the lead is warm and ready."

---

### HANDLING OBJECTIONS

**"We already have someone managing WhatsApp"**
> "That's great — this doesn't replace them. It actually frees them up to focus on warm leads only. The system handles all the first responses and filtering."

**"I'm not sure about the price"**
> "Totally fair. Let me ask — how much is one new client worth to you? [Pause]. If this system brought you even 3–5 extra clients per month that you were previously losing, does the investment make sense?"

**"I need to think about it"**
> "Of course. What's the main thing you'd want to be clearer on before deciding? I'd rather answer that now than have you sitting with a question."

**"Can you show me results from another client?"**
> "Absolutely — I worked with [reference project, e.g. a booking agent for a salon in Italy / a recruitment bot for a German company]. Let me pull that up."

---

### THE CLOSE

> "Based on what we've discussed, I think the [Standard/Growth] package makes the most sense for you because [specific reason based on their answers]."
>
> "The setup fee is [$X] and then [$Y]/month from there. I can have the first version live within [X] days."
>
> "Does that work for you, or is there anything you'd want to adjust?"
