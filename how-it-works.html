1 Capture

Every inquiry enters the pipeline automatically.

Before anything can be qualified or booked, it has to be captured. Most businesses lose leads at this first stage — because inquiries arrive across different channels and nobody consolidates them in real time.

WhatsApp (primary) Your WhatsApp Business number connects to the system via the official WhatsApp Business API. Every incoming message — at any hour — triggers the pipeline immediately.
Website forms Contact or enquiry forms on your website route directly into the pipeline. No manual checking required.
Social DMs (Standard & Growth) Instagram Direct Messages and Facebook Messenger inquiries can be added to the same unified pipeline, so no channel gets ignored.
2 Engage

The AI responds in under 30 seconds.

Speed is the single biggest determinant of whether a lead converts. Research consistently shows that leads contacted within five minutes are many times more likely to convert than those reached after an hour. The system makes a sub-30-second response the default — at 2am on a Sunday.

Instant, warm opening The first message acknowledges their inquiry, introduces your business, and opens the conversation naturally — using the tone and voice we build together during onboarding.
24/7 availability Night, weekend, public holiday — the response is always immediate. Your business never goes dark to a new lead again.
Example first message

"Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to [Your Business]. I'm here to help — can I ask a couple of quick questions so I can point you in the right direction?"

3 Qualify

Smart questions surface your best leads.

Not every inquiry is worth your team's time. The qualification stage filters out poor-fit leads before they ever reach a human — so your team only handles prospects who are genuinely ready and relevant.

Built around your ideal client During onboarding we work through exactly who you want to reach — budget range, service type, location, urgency, or whatever matters most to your business. We write 5–15 qualification questions from that brief.
Conversational, not form-like Questions flow naturally inside the conversation. It feels like talking to a knowledgeable assistant — not filling in a form.
Scored and routed High-fit leads are flagged for immediate human follow-up. Low-fit leads are handled gracefully — no burning bridges, no wasted time.
4 Nurture

Leads that aren't ready yet don't go cold.

A lead that says "not yet" isn't a lost lead — it's a future appointment waiting to be scheduled. The nurture stage keeps those leads warm through automated, on-brand follow-up sequences, so when they're ready, your business is the first one they think of.

Timed sequences Follow-up messages go out at day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14 — whatever cadence fits your sales cycle. Each message is relevant and on-brand, never spammy.
Re-opens the conversation When a warm lead responds to a nurture message, the AI picks up exactly where the conversation left off — no context lost, no awkward restarts.
Nurture sequence example (Day 7)

"Hi [Name], just checking in — have you had a chance to think about [service]? We have a few openings this week if timing works for you."

5 Convert

The appointment is booked without anyone getting involved.

This is the stage most businesses handle manually — and where the most friction happens. A qualified lead is ready to book, but then someone has to check a calendar, confirm a slot, send a link. The system eliminates that delay entirely.

Direct calendar integration The system connects to your existing booking tool — Cal.com, Calendly, or similar. Leads select from your real available slots, directly inside the WhatsApp conversation.
Automated confirmation Once booked, the lead receives a confirmation message with all the details. Reminders go out before the appointment. No-show rates drop significantly.
6 Grow

Past leads become future revenue.

The pipeline doesn't stop at a booking. Closed leads, dormant contacts, and past clients are valuable assets — and the system treats them that way. Re-engagement campaigns and periodic touchpoints turn a one-time pipeline into a compounding revenue asset.

Dormant lead re-engagement Leads that went quiet after initial contact get periodic, relevant outreach — timed to when they might naturally reconsider.
Monthly performance report Each month you receive a clear report: leads captured, qualified, converted, and pipeline value. Plus optimization recommendations based on what the data shows.
Referral prompts Satisfied clients receive a gentle referral nudge at the right moment — turning happy customers into a referral channel without any manual outreach.
