How to fill this in well.

Rule 01

Numbers beat adjectives.

"Conversion went from 28% to 71%" is infinitely more powerful than "significantly improved." If you don't have exact numbers, use ranges and label them estimates.

Rule 02

No name needed.

"A skin clinic in Lagos" is sufficient and protects the client. Ask for name permission only after they've seen the draft — never assume.

Rule 03

Write within 48 hours.

Memory fades. The details that make a case study compelling — the specific problem, the 'aha' moment, the first result — are freshest right after launch.

Rule 04

One case study, three formats.

LinkedIn post (narrative), PDF card (attach to proposals), and a verbal summary (30 seconds for the call). Prepare all three at once — it saves time later.
