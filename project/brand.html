The Identity

A mark for turning
leads into oro.

Leadoro reads as lead + oro — Spanish and Italian for gold. The identity makes the promise literal: every cold inquiry is raw material to be refined into something valuable. The mark is a struck gold medallion; the ascending points are leads rising into revenue.

Usage

Protect the mark
Do
  • Use the gold medallion on light parchment or photography.
  • Switch to the flat gold or cream mark on dark backgrounds.
  • Keep the serif wordmark — Instrument Serif — with "oro" in copper.
  • Give the mark generous clear space.
Don't
  • Recolor the wordmark or set it in a different typeface.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows to the medallion.
  • Place the gradient coin on a busy or low-contrast background.
  • Shrink the full coin below 24px — use the flat mark instead.
