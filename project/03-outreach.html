C

Follow-up Sequence

After no reply
Day 3 · First follow-up

Gentle nudge

Most replies come here. Keep it short — don't re-pitch.

Hi [Name], just following up on my message from a few days ago. Did you get a chance to watch the demo? Happy to answer any questions or walk you through it live if easier.
Day 7 · Final follow-up

Value + close the loop

Last message. Drop a result, open the door for later.

Hi [Name] — last follow-up from me. I worked with a [clinic/hotel/gym] recently and helped them recover $3,000+ in leads they were missing every month just from slow response time. If timing isn't right now, no worries — reach out whenever it makes sense. 🙏

What works. What kills the reply.

01

One question at a time. End every first message with a single question. It's impossible to ignore.

02

Use their business name twice. Once in the opener, once in the close. Shows you actually looked.

03

Specific numbers, not vague claims. "60–70% of leads" outperforms "lots of leads" every time.

04

Send the demo video, don't just mention it. People watch before they agree to a call. Let it pre-sell.

05

Never follow up more than three times. Day 1, Day 3, Day 7. After that, move on.

06

Personalize the industry angle. Clinics care about bookings. Hotels care about inquiry-to-stay rate. Speak their metric.
